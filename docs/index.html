Algorithms & Slew Fixes

50-Sample AveragePos Intent Switch

Toggle the VCU's 500ms lag snap. Disabling this removes the tip-in torque drop.

Symmetric Slew Ramping

Apply ramp limit when letting off positive throttle (eliminates 0ms infinite downward drop).

OEM Lexus Transaxle Slew Limiter

Limit MG2 to 32 counts/ms and MG1 to 64 counts/ms (per upstream reverse engineering).

⚠️ 1. The 500ms AveragePos Lag

Inside Throttle::CalcThrottle, when PedalChange < 1.0%, the algorithm switches from raw position to TempAvgPos (a 50-sample / 500ms moving average). When you hold steady throttle, torque suddenly snaps down toward the 500ms historical average, causing an instant torque drop.

⚡ 2. Infinite Downward Slew Rate

In Throttle::RampThrottle: while throtramp = 50% ramps up, coming down on positive throttle executes throttleRamped = potnom;. There is 0 ramping downward! Any tiny downward foot tremor or ADC ripple drops motor torque instantaneously with 0ms delay.

📉 3. The 400 RPM Regen Step

At <400 RPM (regenendrpm), regenlim = 0. At >400 RPM, regen limits activate down to -15%. Because throttle is mapped as changeFloat(potnom, 0, 100, regenlim*10, 1000), as the car accelerates through 400 RPM (~3.5 mph), torque automatically drops by ~20% at constant pedal.
